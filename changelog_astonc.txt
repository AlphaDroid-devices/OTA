     31-08-2026
====================

* Disable background music recognition by default
* Build AlphaDroid latest fixes

====================
     30-08-2026
====================

* Charging animation: the stock SuperVOOC animation, rebuilt on the OnePlus GLES engine
  - Real charging ring, drawn live from the charging state instead of a fixed frame loop
  - Waits for the adapter to identify itself before picking SuperVOOC frames, so a normal charger no longer gets the SuperVOOC treatment
  - The wattage mark reflects what the adapter actually reports
  - The animation ends where the asset ends instead of looping into a stall
  - Selectable in AlphaVisuals; falls back to the frame animation where the engine is unavailable
* Fast charging controls in Device Settings
  - Fast charging switch: turn SuperVOOC off to charge at a gentler 3A / ~30W
  - Night charging: caps the current further while charging overnight, applied only while fast charging is off
* Auto-brightness: a bad light reading on wake is no longer used to seed the curve, so the screen stops landing on the wrong level right after unlocking; stray zero samples from the sensor are dropped rather than fed into it
* AOD: colour-balance capture now stops while the panel is off, instead of running for nothing
* Device Settings no longer gets killed in the background, so tiles and toggles keep their state
* Alert slider: an app now launches only when the slider actually moves - waking the screen no longer re-launches it
* Screen off gestures: the gesture mask is written synchronously, so toggling a gesture sticks
* Memory: Dalvik heap raised to the 12GB profile for more multitasking headroom
* Now Playing: background music recognition enabled - the phone identifies music playing around it and shows the song on the lockscreen and the Dynamic Bar, with album art. Switch in Settings > Sound, plus a lockscreen shortcut

====================
     31-07-2026
====================

* Haptics: full rework on top of the stock OnePlus effect pack
  - Every system haptic now plays a stock waveform, on a stronger ladder (taps, clicks and long presses were too weak)
  - Added composed haptics (primitives), so the system can build richer effects instead of single taps
  - Per-gesture customization: long press, gesture navigation, toggles, scroll limits and keyboard now feel distinct
  - Added the stock notification/ringtone vibration patterns
* Ringtones: included OPlus ringtones, notifications and alarms, with stock defaults (Night sky / Light / Cloudscape)
* Alert slider: new "Launch app" mode - each of the three positions can open an app of your choice, and the slider dialog shows that app's icon and name
* Performance: moved to the Lineage power HAL (libperfmgr) and dropped the old QTI perfd stack; power profiles tuned from the measured CPU energy curves, with ADPF support for games
* Device Settings:
  - HBM and One Pulse PWM no longer fight each other when toggled quickly: the panel settles between mode switches and a refusal from the kernel is honored instead of being shown as applied
  - HBM now turns off with the screen, and the tile no longer shows a stale ON afterwards
  - Refresh rate preference locks at 120Hz while HBM is on
  - Quick Settings tiles refresh reliably after a change
* Memory & storage: Dalvik heap raised to the 12GB profile for better multitasking headroom, and extra reserved space in the system partitions
* Auto-brightness: a manual brightness tweak is no longer carried over after the screen turns on again or auto-brightness is re-enabled
* Charging: lockscreen and Dynamic Bar now show the real SuperVOOC charging power, live, with the adapter class (100W)
* MEMC: fixed HDR playback, restricted automatic video MEMC to SDR content, improved frame interpolation quality and kept it engaged across refresh rate changes
* Screen off gestures: enabled the gesture settings, including the heart gesture
